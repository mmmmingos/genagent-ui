One place to see what the agents need from you

Three layouts of the same idea, using the same sample pipelines from the Trillet SEO and marketing agents. Answers you give collect on the step, and the next worker starts fresh with only the locked answers. Iterating on one answer happens in a side thread that is never sent to the worker, so it never fills an agent's context.

1 · Agent stopsA step lists what it needs: say 6 inputs, each owned by you or a teammate.
2 · You answer as you canLock 4 now. The 2 owed by Laurence wait without blocking you.
3 · Iterate on the sideWork out a hard one in a side thread. Only the final line is kept.
4 · Fresh hand-offAt 6 of 6, a new worker starts by itself with a short packet instead of a long chat. Undo is there for a few seconds.
OPTION B · RECOMMENDED

Inbox + pipelines

Home screen. One question at a time, keyboard-driven, oldest and closest-to-unblocking first. A Pipelines tab gives one row per pipeline with its flow strip, and every item shows its flowchart.

 OPTION A

Flow

Every pipeline as a full flowchart, plus your queue. Good for seeing why something is stuck; too long to scroll at 30 pipelines, so B borrows its flowchart for drill-down.

 OPTION C · NOT RECOMMENDED

Board

One card per pipeline, in columns by state. Hides what teammates owe and you can't act from it. Kept for comparison.
